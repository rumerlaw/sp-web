      Questions

      What's a genus?

      Every plant has a two-part Latin name, like Opuntia microdasys. The first word is the genus: a group of closely related plants, like a family name. The second word is the species, the specific plant, like a first name. So Opuntia is the genus of all prickly pears, and microdasys is the bunny ears cactus.

      How do reservations work?

      We can't bring every plant to every event, so browse the whole shop, reserve what you want, and choose the event you'll attend. We bring your plants there and message you to confirm. You pay in person when you pick them up.

      Do you ship?

      No. Plants are pickup only, at our farmers market and events. Check the Upcoming events section for where we'll be.

      Where can I find you?

      Every Saturday, 8am–12pm, at Roadrunner Park Farmers Market, 3502 E Cactus Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85032 (opens a map). For other locations we update the site and post on social media a couple of days before.

      How do I pay?

      You pay when you pick up your plants at the event. There are no online payments.

    Questions

    What's a genus?

    Every plant has a two-part Latin name, like Opuntia microdasys. The first word is the genus: a group of closely related plants, like a family name. The second word is the species, the specific plant, like a first name. So Opuntia is the genus of all prickly pears, and microdasys is the bunny ears cactus.

    How do orders work?

    Browse the shop and add what you want to your cart, then choose how you'd like to get it. Pick up at an event: you reserve online, we message you to confirm, and you pay in person. Ship to me: you pay online on Stripe's secure page. Plants are picked up at plant events, candles and books at spirit-side events, and an order with both at events both sides attend. We can't bring every plant to every event, so your reservation tells us what to pack.

    Do you ship?

    Yes, anywhere in the US except California and Hawaii, when you choose Ship to me and pay online. Plants ship bare-root (no pot) for a flat fee by size: $10 small, $15 medium, $20 large, plus $5 for each extra plant in the same box. Candles ship free. Books can be picked up at an event, and shipping for books is coming soon. Pickup at an event is always free. See Upcoming events for where we'll be.

    How do shipped plants arrive?

    Every plant we ship is bare-root: no pot. Our photos show plants in pots, which is how they come when you pick them up at an event. For shipping we remove the pot. When your shipped plant arrives, pot it in dry, gritty cactus mix and wait about a week before the first watering so the roots can settle.

    Where can I find you?

    Spina (plants): every Saturday, 8am–12pm, at Roadrunner Park Farmers Market, 3502 E Cactus Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85032 (opens a map). Some events are for both sides and are tagged Spina + Spiritus in Upcoming events. For other locations we update the site and post on social media a couple of days before.
